F1veClipper — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. Who operates F1veClipper

F1veClipper is a personal desktop software project. For privacy questions, contact the project operator at kristianclipping123@gmail.com.

2. What F1veClipper does

F1veClipper helps prepare and upload videos to supported social-media platforms, including TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. The application runs on the user's computer and sends selected videos to the relevant platform when the user initiates an upload.

The intended TikTok workflow is to upload videos for the user to review and publish manually in TikTok. F1veClipper is not intended to publish TikTok videos automatically.

3. Credentials and information stored locally

F1veClipper stores platform application credentials locally on the computer where it runs. This includes the TikTok Client Key and Client Secret, as well as credentials for its Instagram and YouTube integrations.

Client keys and client secrets are application credentials; they are not the user's social-media account password. The application may also use authentication tokens as part of platform login and API access. The exact token types and whether they are persisted locally depend on the integration's implementation.

4. How information is used and shared

Credentials and authentication information are used to authenticate with the relevant platform and provide upload functionality. When a user uploads a video, the video and information required by the integration are transmitted to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, as applicable.

Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms. F1veClipper does not control how those platforms handle information after receiving it.

5. Storage and security

Platform application credentials are stored locally on the user's computer rather than being described here as being stored in a F1veClipper-operated online account service. Anyone who can access the computer or relevant configuration files may be able to access locally stored credentials. Users should protect their computer and avoid sharing configuration files containing secrets.

This policy does not claim that locally stored credentials are encrypted. Users should treat client secrets and authentication tokens as confidential.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored credentials remain on the computer until the user removes them from the application's local configuration or storage. The exact file location and deletion steps depend on the installation and configuration of F1veClipper. Removing local credentials may prevent the integrations from working until the user authenticates or configures them again.

Videos uploaded to a social-media platform are then subject to that platform's storage, retention and deletion controls. Users may need to manage or delete uploaded content directly through the relevant platform.

7. Other services

F1veClipper's known external platform integrations are TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. If the application is configured to use additional services, those services may receive information needed for the relevant feature.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when F1veClipper's features, integrations or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
